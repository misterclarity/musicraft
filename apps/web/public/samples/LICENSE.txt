Musicraft's instrument samples
==============================

Real recordings, where a free library has the instrument:

- Brass (trumpet, trombone, tuba, horn), woodwinds (piccolo, flute, oboe, clarinet, bassoon),
  strings (violin, viola, cello, double bass) and organ: VS Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition
  (VSCO 2 CE), https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE, by Versilian Studios LLC. Recorded by Sam
  Gossner and Simon Dalzell, sample cutting by Elan Hickler (Soundemote); the pipe organ was
  sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio. Released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
- Saxophones (tenor saxophone, also for alto and baritone, and saxello for soprano) and piano
  (Steinway B grand): Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL,
  by Versilian Studios LLC. Released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ — the authors have waived all
copyright and related rights; no credit is required, but it is gladly given here.

Rendered notes, for the instruments neither library has (cor anglais, guitar, choir) and for notes
a recorded instrument doesn't reach: the MuseScore General soundfont (version 0.2.1, the
"MuseScore_General_Full.sf2" file of Debian's musescore-general-soundfont-lossless package),
rendered with FluidSynth.

apps/web/scripts/build-samples.py made these files from the sources above: soft and loud
recordings of each note it has, trimmed, mixed to mono, levelled, tuned, looped where sustained,
and encoded as MP3. manifest.json lists, for each instrument, the sources of its notes.

The MuseScore General soundfont's licence and credits follow, as distributed with it.
